I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Department of Geography](https://geography.dartmouth.edu/) at Dartmouth College, where I work with [Justin Mankin](https://jsmankin.github.io/). My research focuses on developing sustainable, equitable, and economically viable solutions to mitigate the drivers and risks of anthropogenic climate change. More specifically, my research covers climate attribution, decarbonization, carbon removal, and climate risk management.

I completed my Ph.D. in Earth System Science at the University of California, Irvine, under the supervision of [Steven J. Davis](https://earth.stanford.edu/people/steven-davis). My dissertation examined constraints to climate change mitigation and adaptation, with a focus on energy systems and decarbonization. Before joining Dartmouth, I held postdoctoral positions at UCLA as a UC President's Postdoctoral Fellow with [Elsa Ordway](https://ordwaylab.com/), and at Rutgers University with [Robert Kopp](https://www.bobkopp.net/) and Kelly McCusker.

My work has been published in *Nature Sustainability*, *Science*, *Environmental Research Letters*, and other leading journals. I am also deeply committed to mentorship, equity in STEM, and science communication across policy and community audiences.
